## Security (anything found, even if outside this story)
- S-2 strictly enforced: `SSHTunnelConfig` explicitly raises ValueError if `bind_address` is not `127.0.0.1` or `localhost`. Binding to `0.0.0.0` or open network interfaces is completely prevented.
- Generated systemd unit reads environment from `~/.locus/.env` (mode 0600) rather than embedding inline credentials.
