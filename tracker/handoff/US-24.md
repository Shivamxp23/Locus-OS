## Security (anything found, even if outside this story)
- Extension hook execution is isolated: unhandled exceptions in third-party or experimental extensions cannot crash the supervisor daemon or the agent session.
- Sidecar routes bind strictly to loopback (`127.0.0.1`), never `0.0.0.0`.
- All endpoints enforce the 503 kill-switch when disabled.
