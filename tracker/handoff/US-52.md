- Pi table:
  - Need: Temporal Hebbian learning that dynamically strengthens paths leading to positive outcomes and depresses paths leading to errors.
  - Pi mechanism: Pi has no graph plasticity, no edge weights, and no causality-based temporal learning mechanism.

  - Why Pi's is not enough: Without dynamic edge reinforcement, knowledge graphs treat all relations uniformly regardless of past reasoning success or failure, causing repeated retrieval of misleading associations.
