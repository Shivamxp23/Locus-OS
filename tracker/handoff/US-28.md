  - Need: Session-wide rate limit tracking, countdown timers, and pre-call guard throttling.
  - Pi mechanism: `packages/coding-agent/src/extensions/llama/huggingface.ts:90`, `packages/ai/src/api/openai-codex-responses.ts:123`.
