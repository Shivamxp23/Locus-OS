## Plan (written before coding)
- Implement `sidecar/service.py`:
  - `HeadlessService` supervisor managing FastAPI sidecar (bound to 127.0.0.1:8420 per §02 architecture lock), Pi bridge, and `SchedulerProvider`.
  - Provide HTTP endpoints `/health`, `/jobs`, `/jobs/{job_id}`, `/jobs/{job_id}/trigger`, and `/shutdown`.
  - Clean lifecycle: `start()` initializes the scheduler and provider registry, `stop()` shuts down scheduler workers and flushes in-flight state.
- Implement SQLite job persistence in `sidecar/scheduler.py`:
  - Persist jobs in `LOCUS_HOME/scheduler.db` with table `jobs`.
  - Calculate `next_run_time` for cron expressions using APScheduler `CronTrigger.from_crontab`.
  - Support service restarts: reload jobs and execution schedules from SQLite on startup without requiring YAML re-parsing.
  - On job trigger, update `last_run_time` and recalculate `next_run_time` in SQLite.
- Tests:
  - Add `tests/integration/test_service_restart.py` asserting headless service startup, SQLite persistence across service restart, schedule retention, and graceful shutdown.

  - Update `tracker/features.yaml` for F-0058 to include `sidecar/service.py`, `HeadlessService` symbol, and `tests/integration/test_service_restart.py` integration evidence to achieve Level 4 (`INTEGRATED`).
