## Discard table
- Discarded pure random Gaussian vectors without unitary normalization in frequency domain: pure Gaussian random vectors in $D=2048$ have finite sample variance that results in unbinding cosine similarity ~ $1/\sqrt{2} \approx 0.707$, fluctuating around the $0.70$ threshold. Frequency-domain unitary normalization preserves zero-mean and variance $1/D$ while guaranteeing exact invertibility (similarity $> 0.90$ for unbinding).
